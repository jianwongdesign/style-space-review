Good questions. Clearer decisions.

Understand the offer before you take the next step.

Is S$11,988 a full condo renovation?

The itemised offer is a carpentry and finishing package: foyer, living, master bedroom and study carpentry, plus the listed lighting points, ceilings, painting, cleaning and floor protection. Do not treat it as an all-inclusive full renovation.

Is the advertised price my final renovation cost?

It is the published price for the listed package and flat type. Your final quotation depends on the agreed scope, measurements, materials and any additions. Confirm taxes and all inclusions in writing before you commit.

Are new floors, bathrooms or kitchen cabinets included?

These are not listed in the itemised S$11,988 offer. Discuss these as additional works and ask for a written scope and price.

How long will the renovation take?

The completion date needs to be agreed after the team reviews your scope, site conditions and access requirements. Share your preferred start and move-in dates during the consultation.

I’m not ready to renovate yet. Can I still enquire?

Yes. Choose the timing and project stage that best describe your situation. You can also compare prices and read the included works here without leaving contact details.
